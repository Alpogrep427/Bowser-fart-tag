🦍 Gorilla Tag: Bowser's Revenge

Swing your arms, grab the world, collect 5 stars, and don't get farted on.

VR: touch the floor/walls with your fists and push off, like Gorilla Tag. Type a room code on the keypad in the safe zone by poking the buttons. Climb: hold GRIP or TRIGGER (or pinch with hand tracking) while touching a surface to grab it, then pull yourself up hand over hand. Let go to launch. Snap turn: thumbstick. Hold left X or right B for 1s to teleport back to the safe zone. As Bowser, tap right-controller A while airborne to fart-boost upward.
Settings tablet: press E (desktop) or left Y (VR) to open/close your tablet. Random Bowser rounds: Bowser changes every round; Bowser wins by tagging every survivor once, and survivors win if time runs out. Desktop: click to capture mouse, WASD + Shift + Space, E opens/closes the tablet, G harvests nearby trees, B opens the build tab, F attacks.
Survival: hold your left hand against a tree to chop (desktop G), open your tablet with left Y / E, select a wall/floor/spikes/torch/turret, close the tablet, then use your right trigger / desktop click to place. Craft a weapon in BUILD and swing your hands to hit (desktop F). Mini Bowsers raid at night; a giant Bowser can appear on later nights and drops a resource cache!
Quest: collect all 5 gold stars (follow the light beams), then climb the castle tower and touch the golden flag. Bowser will try to fart on you. Dying sends you back to the safe zone.
Note: WebXR needs https (host this file on Vercel/GitHub Pages or use localhost).

🦍 Gorilla Tag: Bowser's Revenge

Swing your arms, grab the world, collect 5 stars, and don't get farted on.

VR: touch the floor/walls with your fists and push off, like Gorilla Tag. Type a room code on the keypad in the safe zone by poking the buttons. Snap turn: thumbstick. Hold X/Y or A/B for 1s to teleport back to safe zone.
Desktop: click to capture mouse, WASD + Shift + Space, click keypad buttons.
Quest: collect all 5 gold stars (follow the light beams), then climb the castle tower and touch the golden flag. Bowser will try to fart on you. Dying sends you back to the safe zone.
Note: WebXR needs https (host this file on Vercel/GitHub Pages or use localhost).
